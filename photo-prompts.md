## SETUP (paste this first)

I'm going to ask you for a series of eight photographs for a website. Treat them as one photo shoot by the same photographer. Apply this style to every image unless I say otherwise:

Photorealistic editorial photography, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm or 50mm lens, natural light, shallow depth of field, slightly cool color grade with warm skin tones, soft contrast, no HDR look, no over-sharpening. Real working people in the home-service trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing) in the United States. Candid, mid-action, not posed at camera. Clean and modern but authentic — real tools, real trucks, real houses. Uniforms and vehicles are plain navy or charcoal with NO logos, NO brand names, NO readable text anywhere in the image. No credit-card brand marks. Landscape orientation, widest resolution you can produce. Do not add captions, borders, or watermarks.

## SHOT 1 — Hero (most important)

An HVAC technician in his 30s, navy work shirt, standing in a bright modern kitchen, holding a phone with a small card reader attached. A homeowner (woman, 40s) is tapping her credit card to the reader. Both relaxed, small smiles, looking at the phone not the camera. Late-afternoon window light from the left. Wide landscape composition with the technician on the right third of the frame and open, softly blurred kitchen space on the left third (I need room for text on the left). 35mm lens, f/2.

## SHOT 2 — The problem

The owner of a small plumbing company, man in his 50s, sitting at a cluttered shop desk in the morning, reading glasses pushed up on his forehead, holding a multi-page printed billing statement and frowning slightly at it. Coffee mug, laptop, invoices, a parts catalog on the desk. Window light from the right, workshop shelving softly blurred behind. Quiet frustration, not exaggerated. Landscape, 50mm, f/2.

## SHOT 3 — The analyst (dark, moody)

Over-the-shoulder view of a financial analyst at a dual-monitor desk in a dim office in the evening. One monitor shows a blurred spreadsheet-style document with several rows highlighted in blue; the other shows a simple bar chart. A printed statement with blue pen annotations lies on the desk. Monitor glow is the main light source, deep navy shadows. Face not visible. Landscape, 35mm.

## SHOT 4 — Reviewing the statement (tight detail)

Close-up of two hands using a yellow highlighter on a printed multi-page billing statement, one page already marked up, the edge of a laptop keyboard and a pen visible. Bright, clean daylight on a white desk. The text on the statement should be blurred/unreadable. Documentary style, landscape, 50mm macro feel, f/2.8.

## SHOT 5 — The advisor (stand-in for a real team member)

A friendly professional woman in her 40s wearing a headset, on a phone call at a bright, tidy desk with a large window behind her. She is mid-explanation, gesturing gently with one hand, a printed statement and a notepad in front of her, genuine warm smile. Feels like a small independent firm, not a call center. Soft daylight, landscape, 50mm, f/2. Portrait-friendly composition with her on the left half of the frame.

## SHOT 6 — At the truck (wide)

Early morning on a suburban street. An electrician in his 30s and an older shop owner stand at the open tailgate of a plain navy work van, looking together at a tablet the electrician is holding. Ladder racks, coiled cable, and tool bags visible. A residential job site softly blurred behind them. Golden morning light, long shadows. Wide landscape, 35mm.

## SHOT 7 — Roofing crew (wide, cinematic)

Golden hour. A roofing crew of three working on the roof of a two-story suburban house, and in the foreground on the ground a foreman in a charcoal shirt checking his phone with a satisfied look. Ladders against the house, a plain white pickup truck in the driveway, shingle bundles. Warm rim light, long shadows, dust in the air. Very wide landscape, 35mm, deep scene.

## SHOT 8 — Porch payment (services card)

Close-up on a front porch in bright daylight: a homeowner's hand tapping a credit card on a small handheld payment terminal held by a technician in a navy shirt. Only hands, forearms and the terminal in focus; the porch and front door softly blurred. No brand marks on the card or terminal. Landscape, 50mm, f/2.

## SHOT 9 — Van at dusk (background for the closing section)

A plain navy service van parked in a residential driveway at dusk, porch light on, blue-hour sky, very low light, desaturated and moody. No people, no text. Simple composition with lots of dark negative space in the upper two-thirds. Wide landscape, 35mm.
