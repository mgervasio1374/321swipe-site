# Photo prompts — Savings by Caryn (/savingsbycaryn)

Seven images. Save each as `public/photos/<name>.jpg` (landscape, at least 1800px wide, JPEG quality ~80) and rebuild; until a file exists the page shows a placeholder with the brief.

Paste the **Style block** at the top of every prompt so the set matches the rest of the site.

**Style block**
> Photorealistic editorial photograph, full-frame 35mm look, natural light, shallow depth of field, muted warm colour grade with cool navy shadows, no text or logos anywhere, no watermarks, faces natural and unposed, candid documentary feel, Lancaster County / central Pennsylvania setting. Landscape orientation, 3:2.

## caryn-hero  (hero, right side — subject should sit in the RIGHT two-thirds; the left edge is clipped by the copy)
Roadside produce stand in Lancaster County on a summer morning. A woman in her forties in a plain dress and apron, hair covered, holds out a small handheld card reader as a customer taps a card. Wooden crates of peaches and sweet corn in front, a white bank barn and rolling fields softly out of focus behind. Warm low sun from the left. The two people are on the right side of the frame with open sky and field on the left.

## caryn-farm-market  (industry tile, 3:2, faces can be small)
Busy farm market checkout under a timber roof: baskets of tomatoes, sweet corn and cut flowers on the counter, a young cashier handing a receipt to an older customer, a second customer waiting with a flat of strawberries. Bright, cheerful morning light, wide shot.

## caryn-sheds  (industry tile)
Display lot of Amish-built storage sheds and a small barn in a row, a salesman in a work shirt holding a tablet walking a couple past a red-roofed model. Late-afternoon light, mown grass, farmland and a treeline behind. Wide shot, people mid-frame and small.

## caryn-machine-shop  (industry tile)
Parts counter inside a hydraulic repair shop: hoses, fittings and cylinders on a pegboard wall, a technician in a navy work shirt on the phone while keying a card payment into a countertop terminal. Clean fluorescent light mixed with daylight from an open bay door. Medium shot.

## caryn-equipment  (industry tile)
Trailer and equipment dealership: a gravel lot with utility trailers, a compact tractor and a skid steer, a customer at the service window handing a card to a woman inside. Overcast bright sky, wide shot with the dealership building on the right.

## caryn-country-store  (industry tile)
Country store counter with a glass case of whoopie pies and shoofly pie, bulk-food shelves behind, a young cashier in a head covering passing a receipt to a customer holding a paper bag. Warm wood, morning light through the front windows. Medium shot.

## caryn-ministry  (industry tile)
Plain church fellowship hall: two volunteers, one older man and one woman, at a folding table with a laptop, a small card reader and stacks of envelopes, sorting donations. Soft window light, white walls, calm and simple. Medium-wide shot.
